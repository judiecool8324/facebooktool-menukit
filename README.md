## 📝 Scheduling and Distributed Posts

Scheduled posts let you prepare content ahead of time and let facebooktool-menukit publish it later. This is useful for page updates, personal account announcements, group reminders, and regular community posts. You can write the post, choose the timing, and keep your message ready without sitting at the computer when it should go live.

Distributed posts help spread content across a longer period. Instead of publishing several posts at once, you can arrange them so each one appears at a different time. This keeps your Facebook activity more natural and helps you manage a content plan without rushing.

The menu kit keeps scheduling options together. You can review upcoming posts, adjust timing, and remove items you no longer need. For personal accounts, this makes it easier to maintain a steady posting routine while keeping each post separate.

## 👥 Group Social Scraping and Follower Monitoring

Group social scraping is designed for collecting public group information that helps you understand community activity. You can gather details from Facebook user groups and review the results inside the app. This is helpful when you manage multiple groups, track discussion topics, or study how a community changes over time.

Follower monitoring gives you a clearer view of audience changes. You can check follower activity and keep an eye on movement without manually visiting profile pages again and again. The tool organizes this information so you can spot patterns and decide what content works best.

These features are useful for community managers, page owners, and anyone who handles Facebook groups regularly. The goal is to reduce repeated clicking and make routine research easier to review.
